# Copywriting style

Help visitors understand the work, projects, and ways to get in touch.
Use these rules alongside the [design guidelines](design-guidelines.md).

## Voice

- Write in plain, conversational English and German. Contractions are welcome in English.
- Use informal "du" in German. Translate naturally while preserving the meaning and tone.
- Use first person for individual work and "we" for team work.
- Prefer concrete actions and outcomes over buzzwords and broad claims.
- Keep the tone calm and direct. Avoid sales pitches, cute slogans, and CV language.
- Make only supported claims; do not invent achievements or exaggerate impact.

## Structure

- Lead with the main point. Keep the introduction to one short statement.
- Use short paragraphs and familiar words. Include technical detail only when useful.
- Give each section a distinct purpose; remove repeated introductions and descriptions.
- Describe projects by what they do and provide a link.
- Use clear labels such as "Work & interests", "Personal project", and "Get in touch".
- Keep contact invitations brief.

## Privacy and review

- Include only personal details explicitly intended for publication.
- Do not publish private notes, background material, or feedback history.
- Avoid repeating the owner's name throughout the page.
- Read drafts aloud and remove filler, repetition, and unnecessary personal detail.
